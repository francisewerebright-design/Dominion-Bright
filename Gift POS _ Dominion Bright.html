Gift POS

A flyer for a point-of-sale agent, built to make five services readable from across the street.

The brief

Gift POS needed one flyer that tells walking customers what they can do at the shop and which networks are supported, without reading a paragraph.

What the design does

The oversized title and the outlined POS lettering carry the name. Each service sits in its own dark bar so it reads as a separate option. Network logos on a white strip at the bottom show coverage at a glance.

Services on the flyer

  • Withdraw
  • Deposit
  • Transfer
  • Airtime
  • Pay bills
