Unique Unisex Salon

A flyer for a salon in Azagba-Ogwashi that puts every service and the contact details on one page.

The brief

The salon serves men and women, so the flyer had to show haircuts, beauty and nail services together and still feel like one brand.

What the design does

A barber at work fills the top half and sets the tone. The name is set large in white and gold, with a script line under it. Six framed photos on the right show the services, and the phone number and address sit at the bottom where customers look last.

Services on the flyer

  • Haircut
  • Pedicure and manicure
  • Facials
  • Installation
  • Hair treatment
  • Dyeing
  • Home service
  • Dreadlocks
