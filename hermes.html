One bag. One color. Yellow.

A seven-ad concept campaign for the Hermès Birkin, designed so the bag is the only thing in the frame that holds any color.

The idea

The Birkin is usually shown as a status object. This campaign shows it as a color. Every ad lets the yellow do the talking: on a plain studio backdrop, in a craftsman's hands, in a grey stone corridor, on a desert dune at sunrise.

Each ad has one short line, set in a quiet serif. No prices, no logos fighting for attention. The viewer remembers the bag and the color, and that is the job.
