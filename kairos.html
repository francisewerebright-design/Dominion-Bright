Brand identity

Kairos Labs

A smartwatch brand built around focus: the right alert, at the right moment. I designed the identity that carries that idea everywhere the brand shows up.

Client

Kairos Labs, a smartwatch and wearables brand

Project type

Self-initiated brand brief

My role

Brand identity design

Deliverables

Logo, app icon, pattern, palette, typography, logo variations, mockups, social story

The brief

Kairos Labs makes smartwatches for people who want fewer, better interruptions. The brand needed a name people could trust on their wrist and an identity that feels precise, calm and premium.

The challenge

A watch brand appears at very different sizes: a tiny app icon, a strap, a cap, a phone screen, a social story. The identity had to stay clear and recognisable at every one of them.

The solution

A stepped three-bar mark with an angled cut on the top bar, built to read at small sizes. A dark base with one warm amber accent keeps every touchpoint consistent, from the app icon to the mockups.
