Brand identity

Google Pixel Buds

Wireless earbuds that disappear into your day. Nine ads, one clear voice: short headlines, one benefit each, and a lot of space to breathe.

Brand

Google Pixel Buds (independent concept, not affiliated with Google)

Project type

Concept brand identity

My role

Art direction, AI imaging, copywriting

Deliverables

Nine vertical campaign ads with headlines and benefit lines

The brief

Show what makes the earbuds different without crowding the frame: comfort, noise cancelling, sound and fit.

The challenge

One product has to feel right in a studio, on a runner, on a city street and on a bold colour. The look had to stay consistent across all of them.

The solution

A simple type system and a calm layout. Each ad gets one headline, one benefit line and one mood, so the product always stays the hero.

Colour and mood

Night Studio#12141A
Pop Yellow#F8C21B

Headlines use a clean, friendly sans-serif with a lighter line beneath for the benefit. Short, easy to read on a phone.
